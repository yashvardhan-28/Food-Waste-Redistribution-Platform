How Food Redistribution Works

A simple workflow that helps surplus food reach people and communities who need it.

1

Food is Published

Restaurants, hotels and other providers publish their surplus food.

2

Recipients Search

NGOs and recipients find available food using location and category filters.

3

Request Food

A recipient requests the quantity required for their community.

4

Volunteer Delivers

A volunteer picks up the food and delivers it to the recipient.
